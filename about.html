WHAT I DO

Personal service.
Practical support.

I work from my own location using my own tools and equipment. Keeping overhead costs down helps me offer fair prices, while working directly with you keeps communication straightforward.

From cutting and programming a spare key to investigating a fault or programming a replacement module, I’ll review what your vehicle needs and any work already completed. I can help source and order suitable parts or check whether parts you already have are compatible.

For suitable jobs, I can bring my tools and equipment to your location. We’ll confirm the service, location and access requirements in advance. You’ll deal directly with me throughout, with clear information about the work, parts, cost and expected timing.

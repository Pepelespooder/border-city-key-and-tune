WHAT I DO

Keys, programming
and tuning.

I provide spare and replacement vehicle keys, ECU and PCM programming, and vehicle tuning. If you’ve lost all your keys or already have a replacement part, get in touch to discuss your options.

For keys, I’ll ask what working keys remain and what ignition system the vehicle uses. For replacement computers, I’ll ask about the part numbers, symptoms and work already completed. For tuning, we’ll discuss the vehicle’s setup and your goals.

I run Border City Key & Tune independently in Windsor-Essex. Before arranging a job, I’ll confirm whether I can support your vehicle, explain what’s involved and discuss the price with you.

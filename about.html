MY STORY

Why I started
Border City Key & Tune.

I’ve spent around ten years working independently with computers and cars, building practical experience across two fields that are increasingly connected. Border City Key & Tune brings those interests together to help local drivers with the electronic and mechanical sides of vehicle ownership.

Technical work also runs in my family. My family owns a research and development company, and my grandfather worked with several racing organizations. That history means a great deal to me. I want to carry that legacy forward by putting my own experience to use and helping others with their vehicles.

As cars have become more computerized, mechanical knowledge and electronic tools have become closely linked. Finding a fault or replacing a component can involve diagnostic equipment, software and programming as well as hands-on mechanical work. My background with both computers and cars is what draws me to this side of the industry.

The decision to start Border City Key & Tune came after I was quoted $1,000 for a single automotive job. It pushed me to create another option for local drivers: personal service, fair pricing and a clear explanation of the work involved.

For me, carrying on that legacy means making this knowledge useful to people who need help. I aim to get to the cause of a problem, explain the options and move the work forward in a timely way. Whether that involves sourcing a suitable part or checking one you already have, the goal is to help you make an informed decision and pass savings on where I can.

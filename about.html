WHAT I DO

Keys. Diagnostics.
Programming & tuning.

I provide spare and replacement vehicle keys, dealership-level diagnostics, ECU and PCM programming, and vehicle tuning. If you’ve lost all your keys or already have a replacement part, get in touch to discuss your options.

Whether you need a spare key or help with a persistent fault, a few details help me understand the job. Send the vehicle’s year, make and model, a description of the problem, and any work already done. For programming or tuning, include part numbers or modifications where relevant.

I run Border City Key & Tune independently in Windsor-Essex. Before arranging a job, I’ll confirm whether I can support your vehicle, explain what’s involved and discuss the price with you.

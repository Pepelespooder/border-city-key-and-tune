COMMON QUESTIONS

Before
you book.

No need to know every technical detail. Start with what you have.

What do you need for a key quote?

Your year, make and model, whether a working key remains, and whether it’s a regular ignition key or push-button start. If you’re unsure, choose “not sure” in the quote tool.

Do you work on my vehicle?

The services available depend on your vehicle and the system involved. Send your year, make and model, and I’ll confirm what I can help with.

What if I’ve already bought a key or module?

Include that in your enquiry. I’ll need the part number and whether it’s new or used to check compatibility before agreeing to program it.

Can you help with parts?

Yes. I can help source and order suitable parts or check whether parts you already have are compatible. Send the part number and whether it’s new or used so we can discuss the options.

Can you come to my location?

For suitable jobs, I can bring my tools and equipment to your location. Let me know where the vehicle is and what you need. We’ll confirm whether an on-site appointment is suitable and discuss arrangements and any travel cost before booking.

Is an online estimate a confirmed booking?

No. A quote request lets me review your vehicle and the work needed. We’ll confirm the price and appointment directly before booking.

BEFORE WE GET STARTED

A few things
worth knowing.

No need to know every technical detail. Start with what you have.

What do you need for a key quote?

Your year, make and model, whether a working key remains, and whether it’s a regular ignition key or push-button start. If you’re unsure, choose “not sure” in the quote tool.

Can every vehicle be done?

Compatibility varies by vehicle, key system and equipment required. We’ll confirm support before arranging the work.

What if I’ve already bought a key or module?

Include that in your enquiry. A supplied part may need its part number, condition and compatibility checked before it can be programmed.

Is an online estimate a confirmed booking?

No. Estimates depend on the details provided. The final scope, price and appointment are confirmed directly before work begins.

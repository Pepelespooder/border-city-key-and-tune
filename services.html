WHAT WE OFFER

Find the right
service for your vehicle.

Vehicle compatibility and the work required depend on your make, model, and setup. Start by telling us what you have and what you need.

01 / KEY SYSTEMS

Car keys

Enquire about a spare key, a replacement, or an all-keys-lost situation. Include your year, make, model, ignition type, and whether a working key remains. Vehicle and key compatibility are confirmed before work is arranged.

02 / ENGINE CONTROL

ECU programming

Replacing an engine control unit? Include the vehicle details, module part number, whether the part is new or used, and any fault codes. Programming options depend on the vehicle and module.

03 / POWERTRAIN CONTROL

PCM programming

For powertrain control module enquiries, tell us what has been replaced and what the vehicle does now. Include the engine, part number and any previous programming attempts.

04 / VEHICLE SETUP

Vehicle tuning

Tell us your engine, transmission, modifications and goals. Tuning work is reviewed individually; support and scope are confirmed for your specific setup.

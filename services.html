PREPARING YOUR REQUEST

What to send
for each service.

Start with your year, make and model, then include the details below.

Key cutting and programming

Let me know whether you turn a key in the ignition or use push-button start, whether you need remote buttons, and how many working keys you have.

Bring your existing keys to the appointment if requested.

Lost all your keys?

Let me know straight away if you have no working key. Replacing the last key can involve more work than adding a spare. Include whether the vehicle is locked, where it is located and whether it can be moved. These details help me check what’s needed and how the job can be arranged.

Have proof of ownership and photo identification available for the key service. I’ll confirm which documents are needed directly. Please keep your VIN and ownership documents out of the quote form.

Programming a key you already bought

Send the key’s part number, where you bought it, whether it’s new or used, and whether the blade is already cut. Keys that look alike may use different electronics, and some used keys cannot be programmed to another vehicle. I’ll check the details before agreeing to the work.

Before buying: send me your vehicle details and the key’s part number so I can check compatibility.

ECU and PCM replacement programming

Have ready: engine and transmission details, old and replacement module part numbers, whether the replacement is new or used, fault codes, current symptoms and a description of previous work. Let me know whether you still have the original module and whether the vehicle starts.

Programming alone may not resolve a wiring fault, failed component or incorrect replacement module. Tell me what has been checked so far so we can discuss whether programming is the right next step.

Dealership-level diagnostics

Have ready: year, make, model, engine, warning lights or messages, any fault codes, and a description of the symptoms. Tell me when the problem happens, whether it is intermittent, and what has already been checked or replaced.

Vehicle tuning

Tell me what you drive and what you want to achieve. Include the engine, transmission, fuel used, modifications and any current tune you know about. Mention any running problems so I can review the vehicle’s condition alongside your goals.
